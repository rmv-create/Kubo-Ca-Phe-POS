before anything

Getting in

Kubo is not in the App Store. It is a link, and it becomes an app when you save it to the home screen.

  1. Open the link in Safari — not Chrome, not the Facebook browser.
  2. Share → Add to Home Screen → name it Kubo.
  3. Open it from that icon from now on.

The first time

Tap I HAVE AN INVITE and type the code the owner reads out — eight characters, like K7PQ-M39X. It only lasts ten minutes; ask for another if it has run out.

It will say fetching the shop for a moment while the menu, the prices and the history come down. That happens once. You need signal for this step and for nothing afterwards.

Every time after

Your name, then your six-digit PIN. The sixth digit lets you in — no button to press.

It locks itself five minutes after you put the phone down, and there is a padlock at the top of the POS for locking it yourself. Do that before handing the phone to anybody.

Your PIN opens the POS and the receipts. The takings, the costs and the settings are the owner's, and that is not something you have done wrong — it is how it is set up.
start here

The whole job, in one page

Everything else in this booklet is detail. If you only read one page, read this one.

  1. Tap the drink. It opens with its usual size and milk already chosen.
  2. Change anything they asked for — size, milk, sweetness. Tap More options for ice, strength and extras.
  3. ADD TO ORDER. The price at the bottom includes everything.
  4. Tap how they paid — Cash, GCash, Maya, MariBank or BPI.
  5. COMPLETE ORDER. Done. The next customer gets a clean screen.

If the button won't press

It tells you why, in the button itself: "Add a drink first." or "Choose how it was paid." or "Confirm the GCash payment arrived." Do that one thing and it lights up.

fixing the order

Before you press complete

  • Two of the same drink — tap + on the line. Don't add it twice; the + keeps the customisations.
  • Wrong drink — tap the line, then Remove.
  • Change a customisation — tap the line and edit it.
  • Start again — remove the lines one by one. There is no "clear all", on purpose.
After COMPLETE ORDER, a mistake is no longer yours to fix on your own — see When something goes wrong.

online orders

When they pay after, not before

For orders that come in through Messenger, you often send the receipt first and they pay against it. That's what "Not paid yet — send the receipt first" is for.

  1. Ring the order up as normal.
  2. Tick Not paid yet. The button changes to COMPLETE · NOT PAID YET.
  3. Complete it, open the receipt and send it to them.
  4. When the money lands: Manage → Orders, the order is pinned at the top under Awaiting payment. Open it and tap Record the payment.
It's honest bookkeeping, not a trick: the drink counts as sold straight away, but the money doesn't count as received until it is.
the extras on an order

Delivery and notes

Two small buttons sit next to each other above the payment row.

  • Delivery — type the fee. It's added after the drinks and prints on the receipt as its own line.
  • Note (the sticky-note icon) — anything the customer asked for: "leave at the gate", "no straw", "pick up at 3pm". It prints on the receipt. 200 characters.
taking something off

Discounts

Most days there is no Discount button on your screen, and that is how it should be. Kubo doesn't give a standing discount. If someone asks for one, the answer is "let me check with the owner" — not a price typed differently.

If she has switched it on, a Discount button sits beside Delivery, and here is how it works:

  1. Ring the drinks up as normal, then tap Discount.
  2. Choose My own discount.
  3. ₱ off or % off, then the number she told you.
  4. Under What to call it, write why — Friend, Wrong order. She will want to know later.
  5. APPLY, then take payment as usual.

The sum is shown in full before you apply it, so you can turn the phone round and show the customer. Everything is printed on the receipt.

Never give a discount by ringing up a cheaper drink, or by taking the money and not completing the order. Both make her stock and her takings wrong, and neither can be untangled afterwards.

If you see Senior Citizen or PWD in that list, those need the name and ID number off the card, typed in. The 20% is worked out for you and can't be changed.

regulars

“The usual?”

Tap Guest at the top and pick the customer. If they have been in before, a card appears:

  • USUAL — what they order most, and how many times.
  • SAME AGAIN — what they had last time, and how long ago.

USE USUAL puts it straight in the order. + MODIFY puts it in and opens it, for when they want it a bit different today.

Neither changes what is saved as their usual. Only the owner does that.

Nothing appears for somebody new, and nothing appears until a drink has come up twice. That is not broken — there is simply nothing to repeat yet.
who it's for

Putting a name on an order

At the top of the POS is Guest. Tap it to search for a customer or add a new one. Their name then prints on the receipt and the order counts towards their history.

If they only decide afterwards that they want to be remembered, that's fine: Manage → Orders → the order → Put this under a customer. Their visit still counts from when they actually bought it.

the receipt

Printing and sending

After completing an order, the grey bar at the bottom has a RECEIPT button. Or find any order later under Manage → Orders.

  • Send — opens the share sheet: Messenger, mail, save to Files.
  • Print — any AirPrint printer.
  • JPEG — a picture, for chats that won't take a PDF.
The receipt carries the shop's address, Facebook, Instagram, phone and a QR code to the page. You don't have to add any of it.
mistakes

When something goes wrong

Both of these change the shop's books, so the app asks for a reason and keeps a record. Neither deletes anything.

What happenedWhat to do
Rung up by mistake, drink never made Void it. The ingredients go back on the shelf and it stops counting as a sale.
Drink was made, customer wants money back Refund it. The sale stays on the record and the refund is shown against it.

Both are under Manage → Orders → the order → Put it right.

If you aren't sure which one — ask the owner first. Nothing bad happens from waiting ten minutes. Guessing wrong puts a wrong number in her books.
end of shift

Closing the day

Only do this if she has asked you to. Manage → Closing → Close the day. It saves the day's totals as they stand, takes a backup, and offers to send a copy off the phone — say yes to that.

You can still take orders afterwards; they count towards the next day.

please don't

Four things to leave alone

  • Prices and the menu. If something's wrong, tell her. That includes giving a discount she hasn't agreed to.
  • Recipes and ingredients. They decide what every drink costs her.
  • Settings. Especially anything about tax or receipts.
  • Backup → Load a file. That replaces the whole shop with an older copy. There is almost never a reason for you to touch it.

Your PIN only opens the POS anyway — most of the above will ask for hers.
